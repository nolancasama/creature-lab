Verdi, Messa da Requiem - "Dies irae" (part 1).

  NBC Symphony Orchestra and the Robert Shaw Chorale, conducted by Arturo
  Toscanini, with Herva Nelli (soprano), Fedora Barbieri (mezzo-soprano),
  Giuseppe Di Stefano (tenor) and Cesare Siepi (bass). Recorded from the
  broadcast performance of 27 January 1951 at Carnegie Hall, New York.
  Issued 1954, RCA Victor Red Seal LM-6018.

  Held by the Sibelius Academy Library, University of the Arts Helsinki.

This is the first 75 seconds of the source, cut at MP3 frame boundaries so it is
not re-encoded and loses no quality, and looped in code. The source track is
9'54" and 9.5MB against a game that packs to about 2MB, and nothing plays past
the naming screen, so shipping it whole would have quadrupled the download for
audio no student ever reaches.

Note for anyone shrinking it further: it is a 1951 broadcast transcription, so
its real audio bandwidth is narrow and it survives low bitrates far better than
a modern recording would. It is currently 128 kbps stereo, which is generous for
a bed that plays under a child's recorded voice.
