dies_irae.mp3

The transformation music. The first 75 seconds of "dies irae.mp3", supplied by
the project owner, cut at MP3 frame boundaries so it is not re-encoded and loses
no quality. The source ID3 tag (artwork and metadata the game never reads) was
dropped with it.

Why trimmed: the source is 9 minutes 55 seconds and 9.5MB, against a whole game
that packs to about 2MB. Nothing plays past the naming screen, and the clip
loops, so the extra 8 minutes would have quadrupled the download for audio no
student ever reaches.
